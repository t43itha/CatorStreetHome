Who it suits

For landlords who manage everything except the rent.

Rent Collection separates payment administration from day-to-day property management. It suits landlords who are comfortable dealing with tenants and maintenance but want regular payment oversight.

Funds are transferred after receipt from the tenant. Property inspections, maintenance coordination and general tenant management are not included.

Questions

Know what is included.

Is the rent guaranteed?

No. Rent Collection is an administration service. We transfer funds once they are received from the tenant.

Do you manage repairs?

No. The landlord remains the contact for repairs and day-to-day tenancy matters. Choose Full Management for broader support.

Can you take over an existing tenancy?

Usually, subject to a review of the tenancy documents, payment arrangements and the agreed start date.
