Who it suits

For investors buying with a rental plan.

Property Sourcing is for investors who want a defined search, a practical review of each opportunity and support through negotiation.

Questions

Before the search starts.

Do you guarantee a return?

No. We provide analysis based on available information, but market conditions, costs and rental performance can change.

Is legal or mortgage advice included?

No. Buyers should appoint their own solicitor, surveyor, tax adviser and mortgage professional.

Can you let the property after purchase?

Yes. Tenant Find is included in the sourcing package, and Full Management is available if required.
