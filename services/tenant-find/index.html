Who it suits

For landlords who want to stay in control.

Tenant Find is for landlords who will manage the tenancy themselves but want professional help with the marketing and move-in.

You receive a ready-to-manage tenancy with the agreed checks and paperwork completed. Ongoing rent collection, maintenance and tenant management are not included.
